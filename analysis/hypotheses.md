## Scoring guidance for the planner

- Prefer experiments with **falsifiable predictions in ≤8h**. Hypotheses that can't be killed inside one planner cycle become circling traps.
- Penalize duplicates of `dropped` hypotheses unless the new path has a materially different mechanism or substrate. State the difference explicitly.
- A `backlog` entry without a falsification criterion is malformed — return it to the planner.
- When the divergence rule fires, pick the highest `predicted Δmetric / est cost` ratio.
